Privacy Policy for JTools

Last Updated: August 10, 2026

jTool ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide synchronization features between users in the same group and personal productivity tools, we collect the following information via Google Firebase:

  • Personal Identifiers: Your name and email address provided during Google Sign-In, and an optional public profile display name and calendar email.
  • User-Generated Content: Data you enter into the App, including shopping list items, notes, links, cleaning tasks, calendar events (and comments), work history and schedules, tracker metrics, vaults and document metadata (including references to external files), and support requests.
  • Device & Diagnostic Information: If the App crashes or you submit a support request, we may collect technical information to diagnose the issue. This includes your device model, operating system version, app version, and the crash log.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Synchronization & Collaboration: We use your email, display name, and unique user ID to identify you within your selected sync groups so that other members can see who added or updated items, and to manage group invitations.
  • Functionality: Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (such as Firestore) to ensure your lists, tasks, notes, calendar events, and other app data are available across all your devices and shared with your group members where applicable.
  • Customer Support & App Improvement: To communicate with you, process your support requests, and use diagnostic data to fix bugs and improve the App's stability.

3. Data Sharing

  • Group Members: Your display name and the content you explicitly create or share within a group (e.g., shared tasks, notes, calendar extensions, links, vaults) are visible to other members of the groups you join.
  • Service Providers: We use Google Firebase (for authentication, databases, and backend services) and Google Play Services (for authentication). Google's use of this data is governed by their own privacy policies.
  • No Third-Party Sales: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Group Data: You can leave a group at any time, which stops your active profile from being shared with that group. Any historical data you generated (e.g., cleaning tasks, shopping items, notes, links) will remain inside the group for other members to use.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can quickly and easily request account and data deletion directly through the account management features within the App. Additionally, all requests regarding deleting your account or data can be directed to the developer's email: jtools.app@yahoo.com.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures provided by Google Firebase to protect your data during transit and at rest. Access to data is strictly regulated through robust backend security rules to ensure you only access data you own or are authorized to see as a group member.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the "Requests" / "Support" section within the App, or email us directly at jtools.app@yahoo.com.
