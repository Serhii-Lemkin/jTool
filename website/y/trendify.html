Insightful Tracking

Trendify is JTools's data analysis engine. It allows you to track any numeric value over time and see the results on intuitive graphs.

  • Custom Trackers: Create trackers for anything—weight, workout reps, daily expenses, or project hours.
  • Tendency Analysis: JTools calculates whether your metrics are "Increasing", "Decreasing", or "Stable" based on recent data.
  • Group Comparison: Compare your metrics with other group members. Perfect for health challenges or team performance tracking.
  • Interactive Graphs: Filter your data by All, Last Day, Last Month, Last 3 Months, Last 6 Months, or Last Year to see the big picture.

help_outline Frequently Asked Questions

How does Trendify calculate tendency direction?

When viewing a single metric/member series, Trendify compares the latest entry to the previous entry to evaluate whether your recent direction is "Increasing", "Decreasing", or "Stable".

Can I compare my metric progress with other group members?

Yes! In shared group trackers, you can overlay member metrics on multi-person trend graphs to compare workout, savings, or project goals side by side.
