How it works

JTools Vaults acts as a central hub for your group's most important documents. Instead of moving your files around or uploading them to unknown servers, Vaults seamlessly integrates with Google Drive. You can link important files (like leases, manuals, or shared budgets) directly into JTools, allowing your entire group to access them instantly.

  • Google Drive Powered: Attach documents directly from your personal or group Google Drive folders without duplicating files.
  • Tag-Based Organization: Filter documents instantly by custom categories (e.g., "Contracts", "Receipts", "Warranties").
  • Effortless Group Sharing: Safely share important household or project files with your group so everyone has the latest version.
  • Rich Metadata & Details: Track file sizes, modified dates, custom notes, and author information for every document.

shareSystem Share Target & Guided Import Wizard

Share files directly from any app via Android's share menu. jTools will launch a guided 4-step wizard to import your files:

  1. Select Group: Choose the target collaboration group.
  2. Select Feature App: Pick Vaults as your destination.
  3. Select Target Vault: Choose an existing vault or instantly create a new one.
  4. Upload Shared Files: The native upload sheet opens pre-filled with the shared file's name.

settings Vaults Settings Options

  • Default Google Drive Destination: Select the default Google Drive folder where new group document links and attachments are stored.
  • Tag Manager: Create and color-code custom document tags shared across your group. Includes 10+ standard tags out-of-the-box: License, Contract, Insurance, Medical, Tax, Vehicle, Property, Financial, Certificate, and Education.
  • Local Vault Pinning: Pin important vaults to the top of your list for instant access.
  • Offline Cache Settings: Configure local cache size limits for downloaded Google Drive documents for offline viewing.

verified_userWhy Google Drive & Not Custom Servers?

1. Zero Extra Storage Costs

Hosting custom file servers passes heavy monthly cloud storage fees down to users. By utilizing your existing Google Drive, JTools significantly reduces costs for the end user.

2. Maximum Security & Privacy

Your sensitive documents never reside on third-party servers. They remain encrypted in your Google Drive, protected by Google's world-class security infrastructure.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Consistent Tagging: Establish standardized tags across your group (e.g. "Tax 2026", "Apartment Lease") so any member can filter documents in one tap.
  • Offline Caching: Use the cache option on important documents to keep copies accessible on your phone even when offline.
  • Local Custom Tag Caching: Custom tags created on upload automatically persist locally for future quick selection in the filters.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does JTools store my actual files on its database?

No! JTools only stores document metadata and secure references. Your physical files stay safely inside your Google Drive.

What happens if a group member leaves?

Document references added to shared vaults remain in the group for other members to access, while the original file in Google Drive remains under the owner's full control.

How does search isolation work?

The top search bar strictly filters Vault names and descriptions. To search specific files across all your group's vaults, tap the floating magnifying glass icon (FindInPage FAB) to open the Cross-Vault Document Search.

🔴 Red Container: Primary Actions & Background Hub

Primary Action: Houses the Plus FAB to instantly create new vaults and attach Google Drive documents. Secondary: Tracks active Google Drive background uploads via FileUploadWorker, popping up a progress FAB during document edits.
