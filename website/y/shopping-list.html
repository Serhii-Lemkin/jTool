How it works

The shopping list in JTools is built for groups. Whether it's a family household or a shared office, everyone stays on the same page.

  • Aisle Tagging & Filtering: Assign aisle categories (Veggies, Dairy, Pantry, etc.) to items and filter by aisle to focus on one store section at a time.
  • Decimal Amounts & Unit Suggestions: Specify exact weights or volumes (e.g. 1.5 kg, 500 ml) with dynamic unit suggestions.
  • Real-time Group Sync: Items check off live across every family member's phone without double-buying.
  • Smart History: Quickly re-add weekly household essentials with one tap.

more_vert Shopping Item Action Sheet

Tapping the 3-dots icon next to any shopping item opens a modern slide-up action sheet. From here, you can seamlessly edit the item's name, quantity, unit, and category, or delete it from the list entirely.

lightbulb Pro Tips

  • Aisle Filter: Filter your list by specific aisle while in a store section to focus on what you need in that aisle.
  • Decimal Quantities & Unit Auto-Complete: Specify precise amounts like 1.5 kg or 500 ml for recipes with dynamic unit suggestions.

help_outline Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when someone else in my group checks an item?

It updates on your screen in real time and moves to the Recently Checked section (with all checked items accessible via the Inactive filter)!

layers Actions Container & Background Sync

Note: Rapid item entry is handled by the dedicated Fixed Bottom Adding Bar docked at the bottom of the screen. On Shopping, the Actions Container manages background group sync alerts, notification badges, and side-switching gestures.
