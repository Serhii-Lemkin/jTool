assignmentWhat You Track

Work Tracker is designed for precise shift and session timekeeping. It calculates total hours worked, tracks real-time overtime, and maintains historical shift logs. While heavily optimized for shift work, it can track ANY scheduled Google Calendar event or session—from freelance projects and study blocks to team meetings.

Tracking Modes & Calendar Integration

  • Quick Start: Tap "Start Now" for instant unscheduled sessions. Set a goal duration, and JTools will count down. When finished, JTools can automatically log the completed session and shift notes directly into a designated Google Calendar event.
  • Event Linked: Link your tracker to any Google Calendar event. The app counts down to shift start, switches to active work tracking, and alerts you at the finish line.
  • Fixed / Weekly Schedule: Setup your recurring weekly shifts once. JTools automatically identifies today's shift and pre-configures the tracker when you open the app.

settingsSettings & Shift Configuration

  • Weekly Start Day: Select your preferred starting day of the week (e.g. Monday vs Sunday) for weekly hour calculations and shift totals.
  • Shift Goal Hours: Set target default shift goal durations (e.g., 8 hours) for Quick Start and freehand tracking.
  • Weekly Schedule: Define recurring daily start and end shift times (e.g. Mon-Fri 09:00 - 17:00). JTools identifies today's shift automatically when you open the app.
  • Work Shift Color Category: Select the primary color key used to auto-identify work shift events from your Google Calendar.
  • Google Calendar Auto-Logging: Enable automatic session logging to record completed shift start times, durations, and notes into your selected Google Calendar.

Key Features

  • Real-time Overtime: Working past your goal duration turns the centered timer red, counting overtime seconds automatically.
  • Event Ended Status: Displays clear "Event Ended" badges when tracked events or shifts reach their scheduled end.
  • Centered Desk View: High-contrast, high-visibility timer designed for at-a-glance desk monitoring.

lightbulb Pro Tips

  • Dedicated Work Calendar: Select a secondary Google Calendar as your "Shared Events Destination" in Calendar Settings to keep your main personal calendar uncluttered while auto-logging shifts.

help_outline Frequently Asked Questions

Can I track non-work events?

Yes! While optimized for work shifts, Work Tracker can be linked to any event in your Google Calendar or used via Quick Start for study sessions, meetings, or workouts.

What happens if I forget to stop the timer?

Real-time overtime kicks in automatically in red. When ending the session, you can adjust the final logged duration before saving to your calendar.

How does Google Calendar auto-logging work?

When enabled in General Settings, closing a completed session prompts you to log the exact start time, duration, and optional notes directly to your selected Google Calendar.
