While heavily optimized for shift work, it can track ANY scheduled Google Calendar event or session—from freelance projects and study blocks to team meetings.

Tracking Modes & Calendar Integration

  • Quick Start: Tap "Start" for instant unscheduled sessions. Set a goal duration, and JTools immediately creates a Google Calendar event and switches to active tracking. When you tap Finish, the event's end time and a finish timestamp note are updated automatically.
  • Event Mode: Link your tracker to any Google Calendar event. The app counts down to shift start, switches to active work tracking, and alerts you at the finish line.

settingsSettings & Shift Configuration

  • Work Week Starts On: Select your preferred starting day of the week (e.g. Monday vs Sunday) for organizing your Shift Goals and Edit Shifts screens.
  • Default Work Shift Name: Customize the default title applied to newly created calendar work shifts.
  • Set Work Hours (Shift Goals): Configure per-day target shift hours and minutes (or mark Days Off) for each day of the week.
  • Set Schedule (Edit Shifts): Define and push shifts to Google Calendar week-by-week, including a week switcher and "Copy from Previous Week" shortcut.
  • Work Shift Color Category: Select the primary color key used to auto-identify work shift events from your Google Calendar.

Key Features

  • Real-time Overtime: Working past your goal duration turns the centered timer red, counting overtime seconds automatically.
  • Event Ended Status: Displays clear "Event Ended" badges when tracked events or shifts reach their scheduled end.
  • Centered Desk View: High-contrast, high-visibility timer designed for at-a-glance desk monitoring.

lightbulb Pro Tips

  • Dedicated Work Calendar: Select a secondary Google Calendar as your "Events Destination Calendar" in Calendar Settings to keep your main personal calendar uncluttered while logging shifts.

help_outline Frequently Asked Questions

Can I track non-work events?

Yes! While optimized for work shifts, Workday can be linked to any event in your Google Calendar or used via Quick Start for study sessions, meetings, or workouts.

What happens if I forget to stop the timer?

Real-time overtime kicks in automatically in red. Tapping Finish updates the Google Calendar event's end time to the current time, which you can adjust later in Calendar if needed.

How does Google Calendar syncing work?

Starting a Quick Start session immediately creates an event in your Events Destination Calendar, and tapping Finish updates the event's end time and appends a finish timestamp note automatically.
