layersRed Container Architecture & Floating Actions

The Red Container is JTools's unified floating action and progress hub. Present across app screens, it tracks background transfers, alerts, and quick actions with bouncy spring physics and side gestures.

infoWhat's in a Name?

Note: The name "Red Container" has nothing to do with its UI color! It is simply an internal development codename / nickname from early building that stuck with the architecture.

touch_appGesture Controls & Placement

swap_horiz Side Switching

Drag the container horizontally across more than 50% of screen width to flip it between Right and Left sides. Your side choice is saved and persists across app launches.

call_to_action Edge Collapsing

Drag the container towards the screen edge to collapse it into a vertical stack of compact color-coded edge pills. Tap or pull to expand.

save State Persistence

Whether expanded or collapsed, the Red Container remembers its active state as you navigate between app modules.

paletteColor Coding & Floating Action Indicators

Every floating button inside the Red Container represents a live background service or action queue:

cloud_upload
Blue Progress Ring: File Upload Queue Displays active Google Drive upload progress while the app is open for non-obstructive phone usage — the app works when you want it to work. Tap to view the Upload Progress Sheet.
cloud_download
Green Progress Ring: Offline Cache Downloads Shows active file caching and offline download progress while the app is open for non-obstructive phone usage. Tap to open the Download Progress Sheet.
file_download
Orange Progress Ring: Public File Exports Tracks file exports to public Downloads storage while the app is open for non-obstructive phone usage. Tap to manage Export tasks.
notifications
Red Indicator: Unread Notifications Displays unread group updates and support ticket comments. Tap to open the Notifications Sheet.
add_circle
Primary Action FAB Triggers primary screen actions (e.g. creating a vault, adding a shopping item, or starting a timer).

notificationsLauncher Header Synchronization

Whenever active background tasks or unread notifications exist, smaller color-coded status icons appear in the Main Menu / Launcher top bar header next to the notification bell, keeping you informed at a glance.
