What's inside?

The Personal Hub brings together key tools that simplify your day-to-day productivity:

  • Unified Notes & Checklists: Create both interactive checklists and text notes. Perfect for everything from "To-do lists" to "Brainstorming ideas".
  • Saved Web Links: Save important web links and access them from any of your devices. Categorize your research, inspiration, or frequently used sites with real-time group sync.
  • Time Calculator: Add or subtract years, months, days, hours, and minutes from any base date and time, with a Reset to Now shortcut and clickable calculation history.

Cloud Security & Backup

All Notes and Links are synced in real time via Google Firebase Firestore under your active group (or your Private Group for personal-only items), ensuring you never lose your data even if you switch phones.

settings Notes & Links Settings Options

  • Note Preview Length: Open the 3-dots menu in Notes to choose how many lines appear on note cards (1 line, 2 lines, 3 lines, 5 lines, or No limit), synced across your devices.
  • Private vs Group Storage: Switch between active shared group notes/links and your Private Group in Group Settings. (Checklist items automatically sort unchecked items first.)

lightbulb Pro Tips

  • Checklist Mode: Pick "Checklist" when creating a new note to build interactive to-do lists where checked items automatically move below unchecked ones.
  • Streamlined Link Filters: The Link category filter sheet dynamically displays only tags that have active records in the database, hiding unused tags and presenting a single full-width 'Apply' button.
  • Link Previewing: Tap any saved link in the Link Manager to launch it directly in your browser.

help_outlineFrequently Asked Questions

Are my personal notes shared with my group?

If you create a note while connected to an active group, it is shared with your group members so everyone can view it and leave comments. For strictly private personal data, simply switch to your automatically generated Private Group in Group Settings where only you have access.

more_vert Universal Slide-Up Action Sheets

Tapping the 3-dots icon on any Note or Link opens a modern slide-up action sheet to edit, copy URLs, or permanently delete items with Material icons and supporting descriptions, while creation and filter sheets open directly without extra header clutter.

share System Share Target & Guided Import

jTools registers natively as an Android System Share Target for shared web links and plain text (while shared files route to Vaults). When sharing text or links from apps like Instagram or your mobile browser:

  1. Select Group: Choose the target collaboration group.
  2. Select Feature App: Pick Links or Notes.
  3. Inline Creation: The creation sheet immediately pops up with the shared link or text pre-filled and ready to save.
