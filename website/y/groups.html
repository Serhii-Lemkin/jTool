groupsGroup Architecture & Core Mechanics

Groups are the core foundation binding all JTools applications together. Whether managing shared family lists, partner calendars, or roommate chores—or working privately in your personal Private Group—everything syncs seamlessly through your active Group context.

lock 1. Shared & Private Group Management

Create or join collaborative groups effortlessly using dedicated Group IDs and invite codes. Every user also gets an automatic, 100% isolated Private Group for personal notes, documents, and private schedules.

sync 2. Instant Real-Time Cloud Sync

Powered by Google Firebase, changes sync live across all members' devices without manual refreshes. When one person checks off an item or updates a schedule, everyone sees it right away.

verified 3. Professional-Grade Accuracy

From synchronized event invitations to aisle-sorted shopping lists, Google Drive document vaults, and work shift overtime tracking, JTools brings unified professional organization to your daily routine.

info What Happens When You Leave a Group?

  • Account Unlinking: Your user account is unlinked from the group. The group will immediately disappear from your active group selection sheet and menu.
  • Shared Group Content: Items you created while connected to that group (such as shopping list entries, event comments, or shared checklists) remain inside the group for other members to view.
  • Google Drive Documents Ownership: Physical files attached to Vaults remain safely stored in your personal Google Drive account under your full ownership—leaving a group never deletes your Google Drive files.
  • Last Member Data Purge: Because group cloud data relies on active membership, if you are the last member to leave a group, the group structure and its synced database records are permanently deleted upon leaving.

settings Group Settings & Administration Options

  • Active Group Switcher: Tap the active group header in Launcher or Group Settings to switch active group context instantly across all JTools modules.
  • Invite-Only Security Mode: Toggle "Invite Only" mode to disallow open join requests. When enabled, new members can only join via direct email invitations.
  • Group ID Sharing: Copy your unique Group ID with one tap to share with family, partners, or team members.
  • Group Member Management: View all active group members, owner designations, and email identifiers.
  • Unlink & Leave Group: Remove a group from your active account list. If you are the last remaining member, group data is permanently purged on leave.

lightbulb Pro Tips

  • Private Group Isolation: Switch back to your automatically created "Private" group anytime to save confidential personal notes, private document vaults, or personal schedules.
  • Quick Group Header Switch: You don't need to open Settings to change groups—simply tap the Active Group pill in the Launcher top app bar to switch context instantly.
  • Invite-Only Protection: Keep household groups set to Invite Only to ensure only verified family members can join.

help_outline Frequently Asked Questions

How do I switch between my Private group and Shared groups?

Tap the Active Group header in the Launcher top bar or navigate to Settings → Groups → Active Group to open the Group Selection sheet. Selecting any group reloads all your tools for that active group context.

Can members of my shared group see my Private group notes or vaults?

No! Private Groups are 100% isolated to your account. No other group member can access or view data created while connected to your Private Group.

What happens if I leave a group?

Your membership is removed and the group disappears from your account list. If you are the last remaining member, the group's cloud data is permanently deleted upon leaving.
