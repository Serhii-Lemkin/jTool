The System

The Cleaning Manager helps you stay on top of those tasks that are easy to forget until they become problems.

  • Recurring Tasks: Set tasks that repeat every few days or weeks (e.g., "Change Bed Sheets" every 7 days, "Clean Filter" every 30 days).
  • Dynamic Urgency: Tasks move up your list as they get closer to their "Due Date". JTools uses color-coding to show what needs immediate attention.
  • Group Accountability: In shared groups, everyone can see when a task was last completed and who did it.
  • One-time Tasks: Easily add temporary chores that don't need a schedule.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I check off a recurring chore?

The completion timestamp resets and the next due date is scheduled automatically!
