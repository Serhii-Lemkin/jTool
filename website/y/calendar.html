How it works

JTools's calendar isn't just a viewer. It's a synchronization engine that maps your Google Calendar events to functional tools inside the app using color-coded event types. The month grid view displays these cleanly, giving you an instant overview of your month.

  • Color-Coded Event Types: Select designated colors to represent work and activity types, enabling seamless calendar management and direct Work Page integration.
  • Dynamic Day Views: Seamlessly switch between a high-level Month Grid and a precision Timeline view for your day.
  • Collaborative Invitations: Accept or decline invites directly within JTools. See real-time status updates from other group members.
  • Deep Linking: Every shared event generated by JTools contains a secure link that opens the exact event details on your partner's phone.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does Pro Calendar replace my Google Calendar app?

No! It syncs directly with Google Calendar in real time. Any event added or updated in JTools appears instantly in Google Calendar and vice versa.

How do group event invitations work?

When creating a shared event, JTools sends in-app notifications and calendar invites to members. Responses (Accept/Decline) update live across the group.

🔴 Red Container: Primary Actions & Background Hub

Primary Action: Houses the Plus FAB to quickly schedule new events and send group invitations. Secondary: Manages notification badges for shared invite updates, event comments, and background transfers.
