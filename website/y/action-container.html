layersActions Container Architecture & Floating Actions

The Actions Container is JTools's unified floating action and progress hub. Present across app screens, it tracks background transfers, alerts, and quick actions with smooth spring physics and side gestures.

touch_appGesture Controls & Placement

swap_horiz Side Switching

When expanded, drag the container horizontally across more than 50% of screen width to flip it between Right and Left sides. Your side choice is saved and persists across app launches. (When collapsed, a horizontal drag expands it.)

call_to_action Edge Collapsing

Drag the container towards the screen edge to collapse it into a vertical stack of compact color-coded edge pills. Tap or pull to expand.

save State Persistence

Whether expanded or collapsed, the Actions Container remembers its active state as you navigate between app modules.

paletteColor Coding & Floating Action Indicators

Every floating button inside the Actions Container represents a live background service or action queue:

cloud_upload
Blue Progress Ring: File Upload Queue Driven by the background `FileUploadWorker`, this FAB pops up automatically during active Google Drive uploads—even when editing existing documents or replacing files—for non-obstructive usage. Tap to view the detailed Upload Progress Sheet.
cloud_download
Green Progress Ring: Offline Cache Downloads Shows active file caching and offline download progress while the app is open for non-obstructive phone usage. Tap to open the Download Progress Sheet.
folder
Orange Progress Ring: Public File Exports Tracks file exports to public Downloads storage while the app is open for non-obstructive phone usage. Tap to manage Export tasks.
notifications
Red Indicator: Unread Notifications Displays unread group updates and support ticket comments. Tap to open the Notifications Sheet.
add_circle
Primary Action FAB Triggers primary screen actions (e.g. creating a vault, adding a note, or creating a calendar event).

notificationsLauncher Header Synchronization

Whenever active or failed background tasks or unread notifications exist, smaller status icons appear in the Main Menu / Launcher top bar header next to the notification bell, keeping you informed at a glance.
