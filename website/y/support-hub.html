The System

The Support Hub allows users to easily report bugs, request features, or ask questions directly to the JTools development team.

  • Bug Reports: Easily submit bugs with steps to reproduce so they can be fixed quickly.
  • Feature Requests: Suggest new ideas or features that would improve your JTools experience.
  • Real-Time Status Tracking: Track the status of your tickets (e.g., Open, In Progress, Resolved) and get updates directly in the app.
  • Two-Way Communication: Chat directly with the developer via comments on your specific support tickets.

Who can see my support tickets?

Support tickets are strictly private between you and the JTools development team. Your group members cannot see your bugs or feature requests.
