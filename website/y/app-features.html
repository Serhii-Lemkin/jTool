grid_viewWhy jTool Uses an Internal Launcher?

Traditional multi-tool setups force users to install 10 separate apps or clutter their phone home screens with endless single-purpose icons. JTools unifies all 9 essential household and group tools—Vaults, Calendar, Workday, ShopList, Notes, Links, Cleaning, Trends, and Time—under a single, customizable Launcher screen. You get a clean, uncluttered phone with instant access to every tool, plus built-in app search.

  • Layout Toggles (1 to 4 Columns): Long-press any app card to enter Edit Mode, then switch between 1–2 column side-by-side cards and 3–4 column compact grid cards.
  • Drag & Drop Reordering: In Edit Mode, touch and drag any app card to arrange your most frequently used tools at the top, then tap Save to keep your changes (or press Back to discard).
  • Visibility Controls: Tap the eye icon on any card in Edit Mode to hide it, or expand the "Hidden Apps" drawer at the bottom to reveal hidden tools.
  • Custom Launcher Gestures: Map 2 custom gestures (Swipe Left, Swipe Right) to open specific apps or trigger quick actions directly from the home screen.

  • Dark & Light Mode Toggling: Switch between sleek dark slate and soft, eye-friendly contrast light mode in General Settings.
  • Accent & Background Color Palettes: Personalize button highlights, progress rings, and surfaces with custom Primary Accent (15 options) and Background Color (6 options) palettes for both Dark and Light modes.
  • Where to Customize Themes: Navigate to Launcher → Settings → General Settings to toggle Dark/Light mode, set your Language or Weekly Start Day, and tap Customize Theme to pick your colors.

  • Preferred Date Format: Configure your default date formatting structure directly from General Settings. All date pickers, input fields, and displays across Calendar, Vaults, and Tracker will automatically adapt to your chosen convention (DD/MM/YYYY, MM/DD/YYYY, or YYYY/MM/DD).
  • Universal Slide-Up Action Sheets: Tapping a 3-dots menu button across the app—whether on a Shopping Item, Web Link, Note, or Calendar Event—now opens a sleek slide-up action sheet equipped with bold Material icons, detailed descriptions, and instant interaction targets, keeping your thumbs in comfortable reach and replacing legacy tiny drop-down menus.

JTools integrates natively as a System Share Target on Android. You don't even need to open the app to save content.

  • Web Links & Text: Share an Instagram reel, text snippet, or browser URL to JTools to launch a guided wizard where you pick your Group, choose Links or Notes, and save from a pre-filled form.
  • Images & Files: Share multiple files directly from your gallery or file manager. JTools will launch a guided 4-step wizard letting you select your target Group, Feature App (Vaults), and specific target vault folder, uploading your documents seamlessly.
