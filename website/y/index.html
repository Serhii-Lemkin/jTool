groups Group Architecture & Core Mechanics

Groups are the core pillar binding all JTools applications together. Whether managing shared family lists, partner calendars, or roommate chores—or working privately in your personal Private Group—everything syncs seamlessly through your active Group.

Explore Group System Guide ➜
1
Seamless Shared & Private Group Management. JTools is built around collaborative groups. Create or join shared groups effortlessly with invite codes, or switch to your automatic Private Group for 100% isolated personal notes, documents, and private schedules.
2
Instant Cloud Sync. No waiting or manual refreshes. Cloud-powered lists, notes, and task states sync across every member's device instantly via Google Firebase. When one person checks off an item or updates a schedule, everyone sees it right away.
3
Professional Accuracy. From precise work timers with real-time overtime tracking to aisle-sorted shopping lists and document vaults, JTools brings professional-grade organization to your personal and shared ecosystem.
